Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

This policy explains how the Arslan TV application and this website handle your information. Arslan TV is a live-TV viewing app for Android TV, phones and tablets.

1. Information we collect

The Arslan TV app has no accounts, no sign-up and no login. We operate no server that receives your data. We do not collect, sell or share personal information, and the app contains no analytics SDK.

2. Information stored on your device

The app saves preferences in Android's private per-app storage. This never leaves your device and is removed when you uninstall:

  • Chosen picture quality and picture mode.
  • Screen-edge (overscan) adjustments.
  • Ad-blocking and pre-buffering toggles.
  • The last channel watched, so the app can return you to it.
  • A cache of channel logos and a local diagnostic log of player events.

3. Third-party services

Arslan TV hosts no video. It plays publicly available streams directly from broadcasters and from YouTube. When you watch, your device connects to those services and they receive your IP address and standard request information — exactly as if you had opened the stream in a browser. Their handling of that data is governed by their own policies.

Channels that play through YouTube use the official YouTube IFrame player. Google may set cookies and collect viewing data through it. See Google's Privacy Policy and YouTube Terms of Service.

4. Cookies and advertising

Arslan TV does not currently display advertising of its own. The disclosures below describe how advertising would operate if and when it is introduced, and apply to this website where advertising is served.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to our and other sites.
  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can opt out of third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info/choices or optout.networkadvertising.org.
  • Third-party vendors not controlled by us may place their own cookies. We do not control those cookies and recommend reviewing each vendor's privacy policy.

5. Permissions the app requests

  • INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — fetch and play streams.
  • WAKE_LOCK — keep the screen on during playback.

No access is requested to location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

6. Your rights (GDPR / UK GDPR)

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom you have the right to access, correct, erase, restrict or object to processing of your personal data, and to data portability. Because the app collects no personal data, there is normally nothing for us to retrieve or erase; local preferences are cleared by uninstalling the app or clearing its storage. Where this site serves advertising, consent for non-essential cookies is requested before they are set, and may be withdrawn at any time.

7. California privacy rights (CCPA/CPRA)

We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined by the CCPA/CPRA, and we have no personal information to disclose or delete on request. California residents may still contact us with any request.

8. Children

Arslan TV is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone. The app includes cartoon content that children may watch; that content is delivered by YouTube, whose policies then apply. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Data retention and security

We hold no personal data, so there is none to retain. Preference data stays on your device under Android's app sandbox.
