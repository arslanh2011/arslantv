Arslan TV

Pakistan live TV channels, free — on Android TV, phone and tablet.

Arslan TV is a free viewing client for publicly available live streams and public YouTube playlists. News, drama, entertainment, documentary, Islamic and cartoon channels, each on its own numbered channel, in a television-style interface built for a remote.

What it does

  • Live Pakistani channels across eight categories.
  • Full-episode shelves from major entertainment broadcasters.
  • Classic Cartoons — 31 titles, with playlists that roll on automatically.
  • 1080p by default, never dropping below 480p.
  • No account, no sign-up, no subscription.
