Content ownership

Arslan TV is an index and player, not a broadcaster. It stores no video, hosts no media files and operates no streaming server. Each channel entry points at a stream published by its broadcaster or at a public YouTube playlist, and your device fetches that stream directly from the source.

All trademarks, channel names, logos and programmes are the property of their respective owners. Their appearance in the app does not imply affiliation with, endorsement by, or a licence from those owners.

No affiliation

Arslan TV is an independent project. It is not affiliated with, authorised by, or sponsored by any broadcaster, network or rights holder named within it, nor with Google, YouTube or Android.

Accuracy and availability

Channel listings are maintained on a best-effort basis. Streams may be geo-restricted, may move, or may be withdrawn by their owners at any time.

Copyright complaints (DMCA)

If you are a rights holder and believe a listing links to material that infringes your copyright, contact us and the entry will be removed promptly. Because the app only links to third-party streams, removal of a listing stops it appearing in the app but does not remove the stream from its source — that request must go to the host.

Please include in your notice:

  • Identification of the copyrighted work concerned.
  • The channel name or listing complained of.
  • Your contact details.
  • A statement that you believe in good faith the use is not authorised by the rights holder, its agent or the law.
  • A statement, under penalty of perjury, that the information is accurate and that you are authorised to act for the rights holder.
